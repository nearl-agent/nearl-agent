Dev log

What we shipped

Every change to NEARL, newest first. Each release links to verified contracts where it touched the chain.

19 Nov 2026v0.17

Real or copycat stock tokens

  • Check now tells you when a token is an official Robinhood stock token, and warns when a token only copies a stock's ticker or name.
  • The official list comes from Robinhood's own StockFactory contract on the chain, not from a hand-typed list. Stock tokens newer than the list are confirmed against the factory on the spot.
  • New page: Official stock tokens, with every address and a search box.
  • The API and MCP return the same result as stockToken.
19 Nov 2026v0.16

Holders, snipers and creator history

  • Check now shows the top 10 holders, labelled (Pons curve, Uniswap pool, burned, creator), the number of holders and how much the top 10 hold.
  • It looks at the launch: wallets that bought in the first block and in the first 10 seconds, how much they still hold, and the bundle wallets a Pons creator named at launch.
  • For Pons tokens it checks the creator's other launches and how they ended, and flags serial launchers.
  • All of it is rebuilt from the chain itself. Backup RPCs that cannot read past blocks are now skipped automatically.
19 Nov 2026v0.15

Check covers Pons launch curves

  • Tokens still on their Pons launch curve are now checked on the curve itself: a simulated buy and an immediate sell of everything bought.
  • Check shows the creator tax, the launch snipe tax when it is still active, how much ETH buyers have put in, and how close the token is to graduating into Uniswap v4.
  • Tokens that already graduated are marked as such and simulated through their Uniswap v4 pool and Pons hook.
19 Nov 2026v0.14

Backup RPCs

  • If the official Robinhood Chain RPC is blocked by your internet provider or busy, the site and the Check API switch to public backup RPCs on their own.
  • Adding the network to your wallet now includes the backup RPCs too.
  • The landing page no longer zooms out on phones.
  • Stats shows USDG deposited in mainnet Earn pools. Earn is marked live on mainnet.
19 Nov 2026v0.13

Guard on mainnet, Credit removed

  • Guard is live on Robinhood Chain mainnet. The Guard page has a Testnet / Mainnet switch.
  • NEARL no longer offers credit or lending. The Credit page is gone and old links go to Guard.
19 Nov 2026v0.12

First mainnet pool

  • A small ETH/USD Earn pool is open on Robinhood Chain mainnet: up to 10 USDG per wallet, 100 USDG in total, 1% per weekly check, three checks.
  • Priced by the official Chainlink ETH/USD feed. All 3 USDG of rewards are locked in the contract.
  • The keeper now also runs mainnet checks when they are due.

New landing page: the live radar

  • The homepage hero is a live radar of tokens launched on Robinhood Chain in the last hours. Click any blip to scan it.
  • A ticker of fresh launches, a scanner breakdown, the full agent trade flow, products, MCP / HTTP / skill examples and who NEARL is for.

End-to-end testing and clearer errors

18 Nov 2026v0.7

Rate limits and an automatic keeper

  • The Check API and MCP server allow 20 checks per minute per user, with a 2-minute result cache.
  • A keeper runs every three hours on GitHub Actions: it starts Earn pools and runs due checks on testnet.
  • Testnet Earn pool #2 opened on a new keeper-owned test price feed.
18 Nov 2026v0.6

Check supports Uniswap v4 and Pons

  • Check finds a token's deepest Uniswap v4 pool, including pools with hooks such as Pons launches.
  • Real v4 buy and sell simulations through the PoolManager and the pool's hook.
  • Works for every token, not a fixed list. Requests are paced to respect the public RPC.
  • Check now searches mainnet only and falls back to testnet automatically. Tokens whose sell could not be simulated are capped at "Be careful".
18 Nov 2026v0.5

Guard, Credit, API and MCP

  • Guard: an agent wallet with daily and per-trade limits, approved recipients and contracts, owner-only withdrawals and pause.
  • Credit: lender pool, backer stakes of 101%, loans of 1, 7 or 30 days at 1% per 30 days, and a public repayment score.
  • HTTP API at /api/check and an MCP server at /mcp with the nearl_check_token tool, plus an agent skill file.
  • Both contracts deployed and verified on Robinhood Chain Testnet. 31 automated tests passing.
