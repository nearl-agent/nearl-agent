Gate I · Fresh off the chain

New pools, minutes old. Check them before anyone shills them.

Every blip is a token with a new Uniswap v4 pool in the last hours. NEARL runs a quick check on each one and marks the red flags it finds. Click one for the full scan: who bundled it, who holds it, and whether you can sell it.

Not a recommendation. Showing up here only means a new pool was opened. Most brand-new tokens are high risk, and a token with no red flags in the quick check can still go to zero. The quick check skips holder analysis; open the full scan before you buy anything.
