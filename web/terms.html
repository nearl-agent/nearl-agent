3. Risk disclosure

Using NEARL and blockchains carries real risk. In particular:

  • Unaudited contracts. NEARL contracts have not been audited. A bug could cause the loss of all funds held in them.
  • Check can be wrong. A safety score is the output of automated checks. It can miss risks, and a token's behaviour can change after it is checked. A high score is not a guarantee and a low score is not proof of fraud.
  • Earn pools can lose value. If the price ends below the safety line, your deposit is returned at the price ratio and can be worth much less than you put in.
  • Guard. Guard limits what an agent key can spend, but an approved router may send swap output to any address the agent chooses. Losses up to your limits are possible if an agent key leaks.
  • Agents. AI agents can misread instructions and make mistakes. You are responsible for any agent you run.
  • Network and oracle risk. Robinhood Chain, its RPC, price feeds or third-party contracts (such as Uniswap) can fail, pause or behave unexpectedly.
  • Regulatory risk. Laws on digital assets and structured products differ by country and can change. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to you.

Only use amounts you can afford to lose.
